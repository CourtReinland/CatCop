How to play
  • Aim: drag anywhere. The sight sits above your thumb so you can see the target.
  • Fire: hold. The sight turns red and beeps when it locks on.
  • Do not shoot anything ringed in green — three civilians ends the mission.
  • Shoot supply cases to take health, ammo and new weapons.
  • Clear an area and the party moves up through the club automatically.
